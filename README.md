# Oaktree Launchpad

Build a premium, modern, highly responsive corporate website for:

OAKTREE BUSINESS SOLUTIONS

Location:

Lekki/Epe Expressway, Ikate, Lagos, Nigeria

The website is for a diversified Nigerian business company operating across Project Finance, Real Estate Development, Hospitality, Oil & Gas, Financial Trading, Commodity Trading, and Import & Export.

I have provided the official Oaktree logo as a reference. USE THIS LOGO as the primary brand identity.

==================================================

BRAND IDENTITY

==================================================

The uploaded Oaktree logo must be prominently used throughout the website.

The website's entire visual identity should be inspired by the logo.

Primary brand colour:

LIME GREEN / VIBRANT GREEN

Use approximately:

#4DFF00 or a visually matching lime green based on the logo.

Secondary colours:

- Black

- White

- Orange/gold accent inspired by the small orange circle in the logo

Suggested palette:

Lime Green:

#4DFF00

Black:

#050505

White:

#FFFFFF

Orange:

#F5A000

IMPORTANT:

The lime green should be the dominant brand accent, but DO NOT make the entire website bright green.

Use:

- White for clean backgrounds

- Black/dark charcoal for premium sections

- Lime green for buttons, highlights, borders, icons and important accents

- Orange sparingly for secondary highlights

The result should feel:

PREMIUM + CORPORATE + MODERN + CONFIDENT + AFRICAN BUSINESS

Avoid making the website look like a generic green business template.
